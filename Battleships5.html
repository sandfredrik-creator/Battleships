How to play

Use the suggested fleet, shuffle it, or choose Place manually. Manual placement guides you through all five ships.

Tap a starting square to preview the selected ship. Green means it fits; red means it overlaps or leaves the board. Rotate if needed, then press Place ship. The next ship is selected automatically. Undo reverses your last placement.

To move a placed ship, choose it in the ship tray, preview a new position, then confirm. You can also tap a ship after all five have been placed.

When both captains are ready, take one shot per turn—even after a hit. Select an enemy square, then Fire. Results appear after impact.

Sink all five ships to win. Enemy survivors are revealed in gold at game over. Request rematches without reconnecting; the starting player alternates.

Scores last for this session. Leaving or reloading starts a new series. Export CSV to keep your results.
